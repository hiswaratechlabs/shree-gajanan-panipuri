No artificial setup. No pretension. Just pure, uncompromised street food passion right at our live stall outside Precious Studio.

Every evening at 4:30 PM, the fairy lights glow over our live stall outside Precious Studio near Tarwala Nagar Signal on Dindori Road. The iron tawa warms up, the aromatic spice blends are prepared, and cold mint water is blended fresh.

Founded by Avdhoot with an obsession for freshness and quality, Shree Gajanan (श्री गजानन) brings honest, uncompromised street food that honors the authentic street chaat heritage of Maharashtra.
